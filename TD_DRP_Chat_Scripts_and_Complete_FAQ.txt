1. HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
======================================================================

PURPOSE
Help a chat agent explain three repair paths briefly, recommend the most practical one, respect customer choice, and document the decision. Copy the CUSTOMER CHAT lines into the conversation; do not paste the INTERNAL AGENT NOTES.

SOURCE LANGUAGE AND DECISION RULE
"Recommend first. Explain the alternatives. Respect the customer's choice."

Before recommending, review the Shop Locator in ClaimCentre and consider:
- Customer location and convenience.
- Available services.
- Claim requirements.
- Whether an Auto Centre is reasonably close and practical.
- Whether a preferred repair facility better meets the customer's needs.
- Any shop the customer already wants to use.

The three paths in the job aid are:
PATH A: TDI Auto Centre (AC) — lead where reasonably close and useful for coordinated onsite service.
PATH B: TDI Preferred Repair Network / Direct Repair Program (DRP) — lead when AC is impractical or a preferred facility is a better fit.
PATH C: Customer's own shop / non-DRP — support when customer asks for or chooses their own shop or declines the recommendation.

IMPORTANT GUARDRAILS
- The customer can choose their repair facility. Do not suggest TDI requires a preferred shop.
- Do not guarantee that any repair, rental, appraisal, settlement, amount or date is covered or approved.
- Present the lifetime repair warranty only for the Auto Centre or preferred repair facility path, and with BOTH conditions: the customer still owns the vehicle AND remains insured with TD Insurance.
- For an independent/non-preferred shop, the shop provides its own repair warranty; TDI's preferred-path lifetime repair warranty does not apply.
- Any rental arrangement depends on applicable coverage, limits and the approved repair timeline. Avoid promising an unlimited or automatically free rental.
- With a non-preferred shop, the customer may need to coordinate repair dates with the shop and rental arrangements directly with the rental provider.
- For an Auto Centre, onsite TD Insurance staff support coordination; for a preferred facility, the facility coordinates with TD Insurance. These are different benefits.
- The job aid mentions cash settlement based on an approved estimate for eligible shop-of-choice repairs; do not present cash settlement as automatic, unconditional, or a guaranteed payment method.
- If customers ask whether declining repairs or taking cash changes their policy, refer to Advice Centre / GIAC for policy implications. Do not speculate.
- The job aid mentions a co-payable cheque path, but does not specify its criteria. Check the file/process before describing it.
- Follow the required internal process for shop availability, appraisal, approvals, documentation and referral. These messages are wording aids, not new operating authority.

2. FAST CHAT FLOW — THE DEFAULT VERSION
======================================================================

A. TRANSITION FROM CLAIM INTAKE
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"Thank you for those details. I'd also like to go over your repair options so you can choose what works best for you."

B. IF AUTO CENTRE IS PRACTICAL — PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"You can choose any repair facility. Based on your location, one option I'd recommend is a TD Insurance Auto Centre. It offers onsite TD Insurance support to coordinate the appraisal and repairs, and eligible repairs have a lifetime warranty while you own the vehicle and remain insured with TD Insurance. If rental coverage applies, the Auto Centre can also help arrange a rental for your appointment. Would you like to use an Auto Centre, or would you prefer another repair shop?"

C. IF A PREFERRED REPAIR SHOP IS MORE PRACTICAL
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"You can choose any repair facility. Based on your location, I'd recommend a shop in TD Insurance's Preferred Repair Network. These shops work directly with TD Insurance to coordinate repairs and updates. Eligible repairs include a lifetime warranty while you own the vehicle and remain insured with TD Insurance. If rental coverage applies, your claims adjuster can help arrange a rental for your repair appointment. Would you like help choosing a preferred shop?"

D. IF CUSTOMER ALREADY PREFERS THEIR OWN SHOP
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"Of course. You're welcome to use your own repair shop. I can explain how that option differs from our preferred network so you can decide. With your own shop, eligible repairs may be handled through a cash settlement based on the approved estimate, and the shop provides its own repair warranty. If rental coverage applies, you may need to coordinate the repair dates and rental directly. Would you like to proceed with your chosen shop?"

E. IF CUSTOMER ACCEPTS
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"Thank you. I'll record your choice and proceed with the applicable next steps for the appraisal and repair arrangement."

F. IF CUSTOMER DECLINES
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"Absolutely, the choice is yours. We can explore a different preferred repair facility or a shop of your choice. Which would you prefer?"

G. IF CUSTOMER IS UNDECIDED
CUSTOMER CHAT:
"No problem. The main differences are coordination and warranty. Auto Centres offer onsite TD support; preferred shops coordinate directly with TD; and your own shop is also an option, with its own warranty and more coordination on your part. Which would be most convenient for you?"

4. FULL CHAT SCRIPTS FOR EACH PATH
======================================================================

PATH A — TD INSURANCE AUTO CENTRE
When to lead: An Auto Centre is reasonably close, onsite coordination is useful, and it is practical for the customer.

AGENT:
"The choice of repair shop is entirely yours. Since an Auto Centre appears convenient for your location, I'd recommend that option first. A TD Insurance Auto Centre provides onsite TD Insurance staff to help coordinate the appraisal and repair process from start to finish. Repairs include a lifetime warranty for as long as you own the vehicle and remain insured with TD Insurance. If rental coverage applies, the Auto Centre can also help arrange a rental for your appointment. Would you like to proceed with the Auto Centre?"

IF YES:
"Thank you. I'll proceed with the applicable Auto Centre appraisal arrangements and document your selection."

IF NO:
"That's completely fine. Would you prefer a different shop in TD Insurance's Preferred Repair Network or a repair shop of your own choice?"

PATH B — PREFERRED REPAIR NETWORK / DRP
When to lead: Auto Centre is not practical; another preferred facility better fits customer location or claim requirements.

AGENT:
"You may use whichever repair shop you prefer. Based on your location and claim, I'd recommend a facility in TD Insurance's Preferred Repair Network. These facilities meet TD Insurance repair standards and work closely with us to coordinate communication, repairs and updates. Repairs are backed by a lifetime warranty while you own the vehicle and remain insured with TD Insurance. If rental coverage applies, your claims adjuster can help arrange a rental for your repair appointment. Would you like me to help select a preferred repair facility?"

IF YES:
"Thank you. I'll record your selection and proceed with the applicable appraisal arrangements."

IF NO:
"No problem. We can check whether an Auto Centre works for you, or you can choose your own repair shop."

PATH C — CUSTOMER'S OWN / NON-PREFERRED REPAIR FACILITY
When to lead: Customer specifically requests another shop or declines the preferred options.

AGENT:
"You're welcome to choose your own repair shop, and I'll support your decision. With this option, eligible repairs may proceed through a cash settlement based on the approved estimate. The repair process, the shop's warranty and follow-up work would be handled directly between you and your chosen shop. If rental coverage applies, you may need to confirm the repair dates with your shop and arrange the rental directly with the rental provider. Would you like to proceed with your chosen repair facility?"

IF YES:
"Thank you. I'll record your chosen shop and proceed with the applicable appraisal steps."

IF CUSTOMER RECONSIDERS:
"Absolutely. If you prefer, I can also review a TD Insurance Auto Centre or a shop in our Preferred Repair Network."

OBJECTION A — "STOP PUSHING TD'S SHOPS."
CUSTOMER: "Stop pushing TD's shops. I'm going to my mechanic."
AGENT: "I understand, and I respect your choice. You're welcome to use your own repair shop. I'll document that preference and explain the applicable appraisal and warranty steps."

OBJECTION B — "I WANT THAT LIFETIME WARRANTY, BUT ONLY AT MY SHOP."
CUSTOMER: "Can my own shop get your lifetime warranty?"
AGENT: "The lifetime warranty in this program applies to repairs through an Auto Centre or preferred repair facility while you own the vehicle and remain insured with TD Insurance. At a non-preferred shop, the shop supplies its own warranty."

OBJECTION C — "YOU SAID FREE RENTAL."
CUSTOMER: "You promised me a free rental."
AGENT: "I'm sorry for the confusion. Rental arrangements depend on your policy's rental coverage, limits and the approved repair timeline. I'll help clarify what applies to your claim."

OBJECTION D — "THAT SHOP IS TOO FAR."
CUSTOMER: "I'm not driving across town."
AGENT: "That makes sense. Convenience is important. Let's look at a closer preferred facility, or you can choose your own shop."

OBJECTION E — "DO I HAVE TO DECIDE NOW?"
CUSTOMER: "I'm not ready to choose."
AGENT: "That's fine. I can note that you haven't chosen a repair facility yet and explain what next steps can proceed in the meantime."

OBJECTION F — "WHY WON'T YOU PAY MY SHOP'S PRICE?"
CUSTOMER: "My body shop is charging more."
AGENT: "I understand your concern. The approved estimate is the basis of the applicable settlement. If the shop has evidence of additional damage related to the claim, it can send that information to the assigned appraiser for review."

OBJECTION G — "CASH IS EASIER."
CUSTOMER: "Just send me the money."
AGENT: "We can review whether a cash settlement is available based on the approved estimate. Before deciding not to complete repairs, it's also important to speak with Advice Centre / GIAC about any potential policy implications."

OBJECTION H — "WHICH ONE WOULD YOU PICK?"
CUSTOMER: "What do you think I should choose?"
AGENT: "I'd recommend the option that's most practical for your location and repair needs. If an Auto Centre is convenient, onsite coordination can be helpful. Otherwise, a preferred shop offers direct coordination with TD, and you're always free to use your own shop."

8. DECISION TREE — INTERNAL QUICK REFERENCE
======================================================================

START -> Review claim needs, Shop Locator, customer location, convenience and available services.

Q1. Has the customer already selected their own shop or declined preferred options?
  YES -> Path C. Explain cash settlement possibility, shop warranty, customer coordination, rental considerations. Respect choice.
  NO  -> Q2.

Q2. Is a TD Insurance Auto Centre reasonably close and practical?
  YES -> Path A. Offer Auto Centre benefits, then ask for customer's choice.
  NO  -> Q3.

Q3. Is a preferred repair facility suitable and more convenient?
  YES -> Path B. Offer Preferred Repair Network benefits, then ask for choice.
  NO  -> Explain shop-of-choice option and any suitable alternatives, without implying preferred facilities are mandatory.

IF CUSTOMER ACCEPTS -> Record chosen path and shop; proceed with applicable appraisal arrangements.
IF CUSTOMER DECLINES -> Offer the alternative paths without pressure.
IF CUSTOMER IS UNSURE -> Explain the key differences and record decision pending.
IF CASH / NO REPAIR / POLICY IMPACT -> Refer policy-impact questions to Advice Centre / GIAC.
IF ADDITIONAL DAMAGE -> Have shop submit evidence to assigned appraiser.
IF RENTAL -> Check coverage and limits; coordinate according to chosen path.

10. SOURCE ALIGNMENT / MATTERS NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE SLIDES
======================================================================

THE SIX PROVIDED SLIDES EXPRESSLY SUPPORT:
- Recommend Auto Centre first when practical, otherwise Preferred Repair Network, while preserving shop of choice.
- Auto Centre: onsite TD staff for appraisal/repair coordination; lifetime repair warranty with the two conditions; possible rental arrangement if rental coverage applies.
- Preferred Repair Network: TD repair standards, communication/coordination/progress support; lifetime repair warranty with the two conditions; claims adjuster may arrange rental if coverage applies.
- Customer's own shop: eligible repairs may use cash settlement based on approved estimate; shop's own warranty; customer/shop directly handle repairs and follow-up; customer may need to handle rental coordination directly.
- Additional damage: supporting information to assigned appraiser; any confirmed related damage can lead to review/adjustment.
- Policy impacts of cash/no repair: refer to Advice Centre / GIAC.
- Documentation items: choice, the options reviewed, warranty, rental, selected path, shop details, Proof of Loss, cash settlement/co-payable path, follow-ups.

THE SLIDES DO NOT ESTABLISH:
- Exact shop names, availability, distance, appointment slots or repair turnaround.
- Whether an individual claim has approved coverage, a rental entitlement or a particular deductible.
- Whether rentals are always complimentary, unlimited, or available to customers lacking rental coverage.
- Exact details/conditions for cash settlement versus co-payable cheque.
- That all shop-of-choice claims receive cash settlement.
- That TD pays every chosen shop directly, or that repair costs are automatically approved.
- Detailed exclusions, transferability or claims handling mechanics of the lifetime warranty beyond the stated ownership/insurance conditions.
- Specific towing, parts, storage, diminished value, tax or other cost entitlements.

Any added wording about reviewing the file, road safety, approvals, costs or availability is cautious agent guidance rather than a new benefit promised in the source.
